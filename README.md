# Dare-Devil
Dare-Devil is a horror game about a girl named Jesse and a serial killer known as Dare Devil.

## Opening Scene
- Jesse is on the couch with her dog, Rose.
- Every time Jesse or Rose talks, the game plays voice audio and shows matching on-screen captions, and important non-dialogue audio (like the TV news alert) is also captioned on screen.
- Jesse turns on the TV. The news reports that the Dare Devil serial killer is in the area.
- Jesse is not scared, skips to the next channel, and keeps watching.
- Jesse falls asleep on the couch while watching a show with Rose beside her.
